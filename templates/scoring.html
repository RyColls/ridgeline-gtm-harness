Trust: a score a rep can argue with

I prototyped scoring because every other step depends on it: routing, SLAs, outbound priority and the SDRs' willingness to use any of it. Ridgeline's reps already ignore one score. This one is rules, not a model, so every point traces to a field, a source and a config version, and the AI only writes the sentence a rep reads, under a check.

Score a new lead View weights

1 · Explainable

Score = sum of named signals. Each reason cites a record field, its source and confidence. Unknown fields score 0 and are flagged, never guessed.

2 · Versioned

Weights live in scoring_v1_3.json, owned by RevOps. Every stored score carries its version, and any change is explained signal-by-signal against the prior version.

3 · Checked AI

The LLM writes the plain-English "why". Code verifies every sentence cites a real, non-empty field and every number appears in it. Fail → template reasons ship instead.
